Cybersecurity Attacks

Phishing

• Manipulate clicking malicious links.

• Download harmful files & Also visit fake websites for data theft.

Vishing

• Gaining victim's trust and urgency.

• Pressure to share information for financial gain or malicious purposes.

Ransomeware

• Spread via phishing emails, malicious links, and compromised websites.

• Cause significant financial and operational disruption.

Worm
• Rapidly replicates and spreads across network devices.

• Makes systems unreliable or unavailable.
